negative filters against the following roles:

.TP

"Engineering Manager", "Technical Director".
.br
.B Rationale:
Automated ATS filters flag graduation year (2025) and reject before human
engineering review.

.TP
.B 2. Dedicated Web Frontend Roles
Exclude: "React Developer", "Next.js Engineer", "Angular Developer",
"Frontend UI/UX Engineer".
.br
.B Rationale:
Interviews focus on CSS animations, DOM hydration, and state management
nuances, where your low-level systems strength provides zero competitive edge.

.TP
.B 3. Enterprise Legacy Stacks
Exclude: "Java / Spring Boot Developer", "C# / .NET Developer",
"SAP / Salesforce Consultant".
.br
.B Rationale:
Corporate banks and enterprise consultancies use strict keyword filters for
3+ years of Spring/Hibernate or .NET runtime maintenance.

.TP
.B 4. Theoretical AI Research / Data Science
Exclude: "Data Scientist", "Machine Learning Researcher", "NLP Scientist",
"AI Research Scientist".
.br
.B Rationale:
Requires post-graduate academic research in model loss curves, backpropagation
mathematics, or PyTorch architecture training, rather than your edge inference
and systems interfacing capability.

.TP
.B 5. Dedicated PLC Firmware / Automation Developer
Exclude: "PLC Programmer", "Automation Control Engineer", "SCADA Developer".
.br
.B Rationale:
These roles require deep expertise in PLC ladder logic, structured text, and
industrial bus architectures rather than software deployment and integration.
